# Landing Page Copy

This document defines the copy direction for:

1. the root product homepage
2. the tenant-branded lab homepage

Use this when implementing the final public experience.

## 1. Product Homepage

Use this on the root domain, for example:

- `yourproduct.com`

Purpose:

- sell the software to diagnostic labs
- generate demo requests
- explain the product clearly

### Navigation

- Features
- Pricing
- Onboarding
- Contact
- Book Demo

### Hero

Headline:

`Run Your Diagnostic Lab On One Modern System`

Subheadline:

`Manage patients, tests, billing, reports, reminders, and branches from one platform built for diagnostic labs.`

Primary CTA:

`Book a Demo`

Secondary CTA:

`See Features`

Supporting points:

- Patient management
- Billing and payments
- Branded reports
- Multi-branch support

### Problem Section

Title:

`Most Labs Still Run Critical Work In Too Many Places`

Body:

`Patient records, test workflows, billing, reports, and follow-ups often get split across spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats, paper files, and disconnected tools. That slows teams down, creates avoidable mistakes, and makes it harder to scale confidently.`

### Solution Section

Title:

`One Platform For Daily Lab Operations`

Intro:

`From patient registration to final report delivery, keep your core workflows in one system your staff can use every day.`

Feature cards:

`Patients`
`Register patients, maintain history, and manage records from one place.`

`Tests`
`Track test workflows, result entry, and report readiness with less confusion.`

`Billing`
`Create invoices, record payments, and monitor outstanding dues clearly.`

`Reports`
`Generate branded PDF reports that match your lab identity.`

`Reminders`
`Send follow-ups, payment reminders, and result notifications more consistently.`

`Branches`
`Manage multiple labs and collection centers under one organization.`

### Outcomes Section

Title:

`Built To Reduce Operational Friction`

Points:

- Faster billing and report turnaround
- Cleaner patient and test tracking
- Better visibility across branches
- More professional patient experience
- Less dependence on manual coordination

### Audience Section

Title:

`Designed For Growing Diagnostic Labs`

Cards:

`Single Lab`
`For owner-led labs that want to replace manual workflows with one reliable system.`

`Multi-Branch Lab`
`For diagnostic groups managing branches and collection centers with shared visibility.`

`Branded Patient Experience`
`For labs that want their own portal, public website, and branded reports.`

### Onboarding Section

Title:

`Go Live Without Reinventing Your Process`

Intro:

`We help you launch with your own branding, staff setup, tests, pricing, and patient workflow so you can move from setup to daily use quickly.`

Steps:

1. `We set up your organization and branding`
2. `Add labs, branches, and staff`
3. `Configure tests, pricing, and templates`
4. `Set up billing, notifications, and portal access`
5. `Train your team and go live`

### Pricing Section

Title:

`Simple Plans For Lab Growth`

Intro:

`Choose a plan based on your lab size, branch structure, and onboarding needs.`

Cards:

`Starter`
`For one lab with core patient, test, billing, and report workflows.`

`Growth`
`For labs with branches, collection centers, branding, roles, and broader operations.`

`Premium`
`For larger labs needing priority onboarding, customization, and higher-touch support.`

CTA:

`Talk To Sales`

### Final CTA

Headline:

`Ready To Modernize Your Lab Operations?`

Body:

`Launch your own branded lab system with patient management, billing, reports, reminders, and branch support.`

Buttons:

- `Book a Demo`
- `Talk To Sales`

### Footer

Product line:

`Lab management software for diagnostic labs`

Footer links:

- Features
- Pricing
- Privacy
- Terms
- Contact

## 2. Tenant Homepage

Use this on tenant domains, for example:

- `irmclinic.yourproduct.com`
- `acme-diagnostics.yourproduct.com`
- `app.irmclinic.com`

Purpose:

- represent the specific lab
- help patients book tests
- direct patients to login
- show trust and contact details

### Navigation

- About
- Tests
- Branches
- Contact
- Book Test
- Patient Login

### Hero

Headline:

`{Lab Name}`

Subheadline:

`{Lab tagline or short trust statement}`

Primary CTA:

`Book a Test`

Secondary CTA:

`Patient Login`

Supporting line:

`Accurate diagnostics, branded reports, and accessible patient support.`

### About Section

Title:

`About {Lab Name}`

Body:

`{Lab Name} provides diagnostic services with a focus on accuracy, timely reporting, and patient-friendly care.`

Optional trust points:

- Experienced diagnostic staff
- Reliable reporting process
- Convenient branch or collection-center access

### Services / Tests Section

Title:

`Tests And Services`

Intro:

`Browse the tests and diagnostic services available through {Lab Name}.`

### Branches Section

Title:

`Branches And Collection Centers`

Intro:

`Find the location most convenient for you.`

### Contact Section

Title:

`Contact {Lab Name}`

Fields to show:

- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Working hours

### Patient Access Section

Title:

`Already A Patient?`

Body:

`Log in to view reports, check billing details, and manage your account.`

Buttons:

- `Patient Login`
- `Book Another Test`

### Footer

Show:

- lab name
- phone
- email
- address
- website

## Implementation Rule

Use this split:

- root domain `/` = product homepage
- tenant domain `/` = lab-branded homepage

So:

- root page sells the software
- tenant page serves the patient and the lab brand
