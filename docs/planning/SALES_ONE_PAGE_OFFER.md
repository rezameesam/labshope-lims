# Sales One-Page Offer

Use this as a simple commercial summary when talking to a new diagnostic lab.

## What It Is

A lab-branded diagnostic management system for:

- patient registration
- test workflow
- billing and payments
- branded reports
- patient portal access
- reminders and notifications
- multi-branch operations

## Who It Is For

- independent diagnostic labs
- pathology labs
- radiology or mixed diagnostic centers
- lab owners who want fewer manual processes

## Main Benefits

- faster billing and report handling
- one place for patients, tests, invoices, and reports
- branded PDF reports and patient portal
- easier branch and collection-center management
- better follow-up through WhatsApp/email reminders

## Plans

### Starter

For one lab with a small team.

Includes:

- patient management
- tests and reports
- billing
- patient portal
- basic notifications

### Growth

For labs with branches or collection centers.

Includes everything in Starter, plus:

- multi-branch support
- collection centers
- custom roles
- inventory
- doctor referrals
- custom branding

### Premium

For larger labs needing deeper onboarding and support.

Includes everything in Growth, plus:

- priority onboarding
- custom templates
- migration support
- optional dedicated deployment

## Onboarding

Typical onboarding includes:

1. lab setup
2. branding
3. branches/labs
4. staff accounts
5. tests and pricing
6. payment setup
7. training

## Add-Ons

- data migration
- custom report templates
- custom domain
- WhatsApp setup help
- payment gateway setup help
- staff training
- custom integrations

## Sales Message

`We set up your own branded lab system so you can manage patients, tests, billing, reports, and follow-ups from one place.`
